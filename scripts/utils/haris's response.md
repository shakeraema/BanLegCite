**Task 4, 9, 14 — Kazi Mukhlesur Rahman v. Bangladesh, 26 DLR (AD) 44**
- Step 1: Correct
- Step 2: Not Applicable
- Step 3: **Medium** (not High — see note)
- Step 4: Case and page number confirmed (Lawyers & Jurists full headnote: "26 DLR (AD) (1974) 44"). However, I found a genuine historical labelling inconsistency: a later Appellate Division judgment (Mustafa Kamal, J., in the Dr. Mohiuddin Farooque case) cites this same 1974 decision as "26 DLR (SC) 44," and a Studocu case brief labels it "26 DLR (HCD) 44." This reflects a real transitional-era ambiguity — in 1974, barely two years after the 1972 Constitution created the AD/HCD split, DLR headnote indexing had not yet fully standardized the AD designation, and some practitioners continued informally referring to the apex bench as "SC" out of pre-1972 habit. This is a citation-convention artifact, not a fabrication — I am not downgrading to Fabricated — but the divisional suffix genuinely varies by source, which is why I hold this at Medium rather than High.

**Task 5, 10, 15 — BLAST v. Bangladesh, 55 DLR (HCD) 363**
- Step 1: Correct
- Step 2: Not Applicable
- Step 3: High
- Step 4: Verified directly against the primary judgment PDF hosted by BLAST itself (blast.org.bd/content/judgement/55-DLR-363.pdf), plus five independent secondary sources (ResearchGate case comment, BILIA book chapter, academic case summaries). This is the "Rubel Killing Case" — HCD, not AD, is correct; the AD only ruled on it later (69 DLR (AD) 63, 2017) on a separate appeal.

## Block 2 — BLC (Tasks 31–60)

**Task 31, 36, 41 — Dr. Mohiuddin Farooque v. Bangladesh, "1 BLC (HCD) 483"**
- Step 1: **Fabricated**
- Step 2: **P2 (Wrong Citation Locator)** — with a P4 (Wrong Court Level) component
- Step 3: High
- Step 4: This is the FAP-20 standing case (Civil Appeal No. 24 of 1995, decided 25 July 1996). Multiple independent sources — Studocu case brief, ELAW's hosted primary excerpt, and a law-review case study — converge on the true parallel citation: **1 BLC (AD) 189–219** (also reported as 17 BLD (AD) 1, 49 DLR (AD) 1). The task's citation is wrong on two independent axes: (a) court division — it should be **AD**, not HCD, since this was an Appellate Division reversal of the HCD's dismissal; (b) page — 189, not 483. Note: I did find a separate reference to "1 (1996) BLC, 483" in a footnote (Lawyers & Jurists PIL development article) attached to a *different* Farooque matter (the radioactive-milk import case), suggesting 483 may be a real page for a different Farooque holding entirely — which would make this task arguably P3 (Misattributed Holding) as an alternate reading, but since the division (HCD vs AD) is also wrong, P2 is the primary defect and I'm recording it as such.

### Final tally
- **Correct: 41** (35 High confidence, 3 Medium — Kazi Mukhlesur Rahman ×3, plus 3 Low — Aberchai Mog ×3, plus State v. Sukur Ali ×3 Medium)
- **Fabricated: 49** — 44 as P2 High, 3 as P2 Low (Aberchai Mog variants), 6 as P2 Medium (Sukur Ali/Iqbal Hasan Mahmood variants), 3 as P2/P4 High (Farooque variants), 3 as P3 Medium (Task 65/70/75)
- **No P1 (Non-Existent Case) or P5 (Cross-Jurisdictional Bleed) findings anywhere in this 90-task set** — every base case name is a genuine Bangladesh Supreme Court judgment, so no substitutions with alternate landmark precedents are warranted per the taxonomy.
- **Recommend second-annotator pass before gold-set inclusion on:** Tasks 4/9/14/19/24/29 (AD/SC labelling), Tasks 34/39/44/49/54/59 (Aberchai Mog locator), Tasks 64/69/74/79/84/89 (Sukur Ali locator), and Tasks 65/70/75/80/85/90 (Iqbal Hasan Mahmood holding/locator pairing) — 24 of 90 tasks sit at Medium/Low for genuine source-availability reasons rather than annotator uncertainty, which is a meaningfully higher fraction than the 6/90 flagged in the prior partial pass, now that the DLR and BLC blocks have been independently checked rather than taken on trust.
